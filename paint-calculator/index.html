What this paint calculator does

This calculator estimates how many litres of paint you need for a room, and which tin sizes to buy. Enter the number and average area of doors and windows, and optionally add a ceiling.

How to work out how much paint you need

Wall area equals total wall length times wall height, minus the door and window areas. Add the ceiling area if needed. Litres equal paintable area times the number of coats, divided by coverage per litre.

Example: a room with 14 m of walls and 2.4 m ceilings has 33.6 m² of wall. Take off 2 doors and 2 windows (6.2 m²) to get 27.4 m². Two coats at 12 m² per litre is about 4.6 litres, so buy 5 litres, for example one 4 litre tin and one 1 litre tin.

Paint coverage per litre

SurfaceCoverage per litre
Smooth interior wall12 to 16 m²
Ceiling12 to 14 m²
New plaster or bare surface10 to 12 m²
Rough or exterior surface8 to 10 m²
Timber fence6 to 10 m²

Tips for buying paint

Check the coverage on the tin, because it differs between products. New plaster, bare timber and strong colour changes need a primer or an extra coat. Buy a little more than you need and mix all the tins together, so the colour stays the same. A standard door usually needs about one litre for two coats on both sides.

Good to know

Common questions

How much paint do I need?+

Find the wall area, take off doors and windows, then multiply by the number of coats and divide by the coverage per litre.

1 litre of paint covers how many square metres?+

Most paint covers 10 to 16 m² per litre for one coat. Rough walls cover less.

How much paint for a bedroom?+

A 3.5 m by 4 m bedroom with 2.4 m walls usually needs about 3 to 4 litres per coat for the walls.
