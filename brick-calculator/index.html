What this brick calculator does

This calculator tells you how many bricks or blocks you need for a wall. It takes off doors and windows, uses a 10 mm mortar joint and adds extra for breakage.

How many bricks per m²

A standard Australian brick is 230 mm long, 110 mm wide and 76 mm high. With a 10 mm mortar joint, a wall uses about 48 bricks per square metre. A besser block wall uses about 12.5 blocks per square metre.

Example: a wall 6 m long and 2.4 m high is 14.4 m². Take off 3 m² of openings to get 11.4 m². That is about 552 bricks, or 580 with 5% extra.

How many courses

Each brick course is about 86 mm high, which is the 76 mm brick plus a 10 mm joint. Divide your wall height by 86 mm to find the number of courses. A 2.4 m wall has about 28 courses.

Tips

Order all your bricks at once, because colour can change between batches. Pallet sizes vary by brick, so ask your supplier how many bricks are on a pallet. Double brick walls need twice as many bricks.
