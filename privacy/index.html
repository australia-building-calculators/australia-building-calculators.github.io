Privacy

A short, plain summary of how this website handles information. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Your calculator entries

The calculators run in your web browser. The measurements you type are used to show your result on your own screen. They are not sent to us and we do not store them.

Accounts and personal information

You do not need an account to use this site, and the calculators do not ask for personal details.

Third party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page opens. The website host may also keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

Advertising, analytics and cookies

This version of the website does not include third-party advertising scripts or analytics tools. If advertising or analytics are introduced, this notice will be updated before those services are enabled to identify the providers, explain the data and cookies they may use, and describe any consent choices available to visitors.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or its third-party services change. The date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
