What this gravel calculator does

This calculator tells you how much gravel you need for a driveway, path, garden bed or drainage trench. Add a second rectangular section for another area with the same depth. You get the volume in cubic metres and the weight in tonnes, which is how most suppliers sell it.

In Australia, gravel is sold under many names. This calculator covers road base, crusher dust, blue metal, crushed rock and decorative pebbles.

How to work out how much gravel you need

Add the two rectangular areas together, then multiply by the depth in metres. To get tonnes, multiply the adjusted volume by the weight of the material per cubic metre.

Example: a driveway 6 m long and 3 m wide with 75 mm of gravel is 1.35 m³. Add 15% for compaction and you need about 1.55 m³. In road base, that is about 2.9 tonnes.

Tips for ordering gravel

Loose gravel settles once it is compacted, so always add 10 to 15%. Lay geotextile fabric underneath to stop weeds and stop the gravel sinking into soil. Ask your supplier if they price by the tonne or the cubic metre, and check that a tipper truck can reach your site.

Good to know

Common questions

How much gravel do I need?+

Multiply length by width by depth in metres to get cubic metres. Then multiply by the material weight to get tonnes.

How much does a cubic metre of road base weigh?+

Compacted road base is about 1.9 tonnes per cubic metre. Loose crushed rock is closer to 1.6 tonnes.

What depth of gravel is best?+

Paths need about 50 to 75 mm. Driveways need 100 mm or more, laid over a compacted base.
