What this lineal metre calculator does

This calculator converts square metres into lineal metres (also called linear metres). Use it for flooring boards, decking, skirting, fabric and any material sold by length.

What is a lineal metre

A lineal metre is one metre of length, measured in a straight line, no matter how wide the material is. Ten boards that are each 1 m long make 10 lineal metres.

How to convert square metres to lineal metres

Divide the area in square metres by the width of the board in metres.

Example: to cover 20 m² with boards that are 130 mm wide, divide 20 by 0.13. You need about 154 lineal metres, or about 169 with 10% extra.

Tips

For tongue and groove boards, use the cover width, which is the width that shows once boards are joined. Divide the total lineal metres by the board length to find how many boards to order.

Common questions

What is a lineal metre?+

How do I convert square metres to lineal metres?+

Divide the area in square metres by the width in metres.

Are linear metres and lineal metres the same?+

Yes. In Australia both words mean the same thing.
