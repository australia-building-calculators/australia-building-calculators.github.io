What this screed calculator does

This calculator works out how much screed you need to level a floor, and how much sand and cement to buy for your mix. Screed is the smooth layer laid over concrete before tiles, timber or other flooring.

How to calculate screed

Volume equals floor area times thickness. Dry sand and cement take up about 30% more space than the finished screed, so the calculator allows for that.

Example: a 20 m² floor at 40 mm is 0.8 m³. Add 10% for waste to get 0.88 m³. With a 4 to 1 mix you need about 17 bags of cement (20 kg) and 1.5 tonnes of sharp sand.

Screed mix ratio

A common mix is 4 parts sharp sand to 1 part cement. Use just enough water to make the mix damp, like wet sand. Too much water weakens the screed and makes it crack. For very thin levelling jobs, a self levelling compound may suit better. Check the bag for how much area it covers at your thickness.

Good to know

Common questions

What is a good screed mix ratio?+

A common mix is 4 parts sharp sand to 1 part cement, with just enough water to make it damp.

How much sharp sand do I need?+

A 20 m² floor at 40 mm thick needs about 0.9 m³ of screed, which is around 1.5 tonnes of sand.

How thick should a floor screed be?+

Bonded screed is about 25 to 40 mm. Unbonded and floating screeds are 50 mm or more.
