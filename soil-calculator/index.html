What this soil calculator does

This calculator works out how much soil or topsoil you need for garden beds, lawns, raised planters and turf underlay. Add a second rectangular section for another bed with the same depth. It shows cubic metres, tonnes and the number of 30 litre bags.

Buying the right amount saves a second delivery and stops you paying for soil you do not use.

How to calculate how much soil you need

Add the rectangular garden areas together, then multiply by the depth in metres. A garden bed 4 m long and 2 m wide with 150 mm of soil is 4 × 2 × 0.15 = 1.2 m³. Add 10% for settling and order about 1.3 m³, which is roughly 1.7 tonnes of topsoil.

How deep should soil be?

JobSoil depth
New lawn from seed100 to 150 mm
Turf underlay50 to 100 mm
Lawn top dressing10 to 20 mm
Garden bed150 to 300 mm
Raised vegetable bed300 to 400 mm

Litres, bags and weight

There are 1,000 litres in a cubic metre of soil, so 1 m³ fills about 33 bags of 30 litres. Topsoil weighs about 1.3 tonnes per cubic metre. Wet soil is heavier, so the weight on delivery day can be higher than the figure here.

Tips

Bulk delivery is usually cheaper than bags once you need more than about half a cubic metre. Loosen the ground underneath before you spread new soil, so roots can grow down into it.

Good to know

Common questions

How much soil do I need?+

Multiply length by width by depth in metres. A 4 m by 2 m bed that is 150 mm deep needs 1.2 m³.

How many litres of soil in a cubic metre?+

There are 1,000 litres in 1 cubic metre.

How much does a cubic metre of soil weigh?+

Topsoil weighs about 1.3 tonnes per cubic metre. Wet soil weighs more.
