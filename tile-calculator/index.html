What this tile calculator does

This calculator tells you how many tiles to buy for a floor, wall, bathroom or garage. Add a second rectangular section when the same tile is used in both areas. It includes the grout gap and an allowance for cuts and breakage, and it can also work out how many boxes you need.

How to work out how many tiles you need

Divide the area by the size of one tile plus the grout gap, then add extra for waste.

Example: a 4 m by 3 m floor is 12 m². A 300 × 600 mm tile with a 3 mm gap covers about 0.183 m², so you need about 66 tiles. Add 10% and buy 73.

Standard tile sizes and thickness

Common sizes in Australia are 300 × 300, 300 × 600, 600 × 600 and 600 × 1200 mm. Floor tiles are usually 8 to 10 mm thick and wall tiles 6 to 8 mm.

Tips

Allow 10% extra for a straight lay and 15% for diagonal or herringbone patterns. Always buy all your tiles from the same batch, because colour can vary between batches. Keep a few spare tiles for future repairs.
