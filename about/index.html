About Australia Building Calculators

Free, simple calculators for building, renovating and landscaping projects in Australia.

What this site is

Australia Building Calculators helps you estimate how much material a project may need before you buy. It covers concrete, gravel, soil, mulch, paint, tiles, bricks, screed and common area and volume conversions.

Everything uses metric units and terms that Australian suppliers use, such as road base, crusher dust and blue metal.

Who creates and maintains this website

Australia Building Calculators is an independently developed website maintained by its site owner. It is a practical online project rather than a building supplier, trade business or government service. The site does not claim professional trade, engineering or construction qualifications.

Why the site was created

The purpose is to make common building, renovation and landscaping quantity estimates easier to work out. The calculators are free to use, use Australian metric measurements and show the main assumptions behind their estimates so visitors can check them against their own products and project conditions.

How the calculators work

Enter your measurements and the results recalculate as you type or change an option. Nothing needs to be installed and no account is needed. You can read the formulas and assumptions on the How We Calculate page.

A guide, not a final order

Results are estimates. Product yields, material weights and site conditions vary, so always confirm quantities with your supplier or a qualified tradesperson before you buy.
