What this cubic metre calculator does

This calculator works out volume in cubic metres (m³) and litres for rectangular spaces and cylinders. Use it for trenches, pits, planters, fill, tanks or post holes.

How to calculate cubic metres

For a rectangular space, multiply length by width by height, all in metres. For a cylinder, multiply pi by the radius squared and the height. The radius is half the diameter.

How to convert litres to m³

Divide litres by 1,000. A 500 litre tank is 0.5 m³. For a round tank or post hole, choose cylinder and enter its diameter and height.

Common questions

How do you calculate cubic metres?+

For a rectangular space, multiply length by width by height. For a cylinder, multiply pi by the radius squared and the height.

How many litres in a cubic metre?+

There are 1,000 litres in 1 cubic metre.

How do I convert millimetres to metres?+

Divide by 1,000. For example, 150 mm is 0.15 m.
