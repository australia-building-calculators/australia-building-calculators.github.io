What this square metre calculator does

This calculator finds the area of rectangular, triangular and circular spaces in square metres (m²). For an L shaped room, choose rectangle and add a second rectangular section. Use the result for flooring, paint, tiles, turf and other materials.

How to work out square metres

For a rectangle, multiply length by width. For a triangle, multiply the base by the perpendicular height and divide by two. For a circle, multiply pi by the radius squared.

Tips

Measure in metres, not centimetres, to avoid mistakes. For an L shaped area, split the space into rectangles that do not overlap and add them together.

Common questions

How do you work out square metres?+

For a rectangle, multiply length by width. For a triangle, multiply base by perpendicular height and divide by two. For a circle, use pi times radius squared.

How do I calculate an L shaped room?+

Split the room into two rectangles, measure each one and add the areas. Make sure the two rectangles do not overlap.

How many square feet in a square metre?+

One square metre is about 10.76 square feet.
