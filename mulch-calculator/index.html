What this mulch calculator does

This calculator estimates how much mulch you need for your garden beds. Add a second rectangular bed when both areas have the same depth. It shows cubic metres, litres and the number of 30 litre bags, so you can choose between bags and a bulk delivery.

How to estimate mulch

Add the rectangular garden-bed areas together, then multiply by the depth in metres. A bed 5 m long and 2 m wide with 75 mm of mulch is 5 × 2 × 0.075 = 0.75 m³. Add 10% extra and you need about 0.83 m³, or around 28 bags of 30 litres.

How deep should mulch be?

WhereMulch depth
Garden beds50 to 75 mm
Around trees and shrubs75 to 100 mm
Pathways75 to 100 mm

Types of mulch

Common choices are pine bark, hardwood chip, sugarcane mulch and eucalyptus mulch. Bark and woodchip last longest. Sugarcane breaks down faster and feeds the soil. Weight and price differ by type, so ask your supplier.

Tips

Mulch helps keep weeds down and holds moisture in the soil. Keep it a few centimetres away from plant stems and tree trunks to prevent rot. Top it up once a year, because it breaks down over time.

Good to know

Common questions

How much mulch do I need?+

Multiply length by width by depth in metres. A 5 m by 2 m bed at 75 mm needs 0.75 m³.

How many litres of mulch in a cubic metre?+

There are 1,000 litres in 1 cubic metre of mulch.

How deep should mulch be?+

Aim for 50 to 75 mm. Keep it away from plant stems.
