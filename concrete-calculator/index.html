What this concrete calculator does

This calculator works out how much concrete you need for a slab, path, patio or driveway. Add a second rectangular section when your project has another area with the same thickness. It shows cubic metres (m³) for ready mixed concrete, and the number of 20 kg and 40 kg bags if you plan to mix it yourself.

Getting the amount right matters. If you order too little, you get a cold joint, which weakens the slab. If you order too much, you pay for concrete you cannot use.

How to calculate concrete for a slab

Volume equals length times width times thickness, with every measurement in metres. Divide the thickness in millimetres by 1,000 first.

Example: a 5 m by 3 m slab that is 100 mm thick is 5 × 3 × 0.1 = 1.5 m³. Add 10% for waste and uneven ground and you order about 1.65 m³.

Tips before you pour

Compact the base and set up your formwork before the truck arrives. Steel reinforcing mesh helps control cracking on slabs and driveways. Check the weather, because heat and rain both change how concrete sets. Jobs under about 1 m³ are often cheaper with bags, while bigger jobs suit ready mix. Bag yields differ between brands, so check the label.

Good to know

Common questions

How many 20kg bags of concrete per m3?+

A 20 kg bag makes about 0.01 m³, so you need about 100 bags for 1 m³. A 40 kg bag makes about 0.02 m³, so about 50 bags.

How much concrete do I need for a slab?+

Multiply length by width by thickness, all in metres. A 5 m by 3 m slab that is 100 mm thick needs 1.5 m³, plus extra for waste.

How much does concrete weigh per m3?+

Normal concrete weighs about 2,400 kg per cubic metre.
